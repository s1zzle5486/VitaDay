# Air quality data

Open-Meteo Air Quality API: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/air-quality-api
Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS), Atmosphere Data Store.
CAMS European air quality forecasts, ENSEMBLE data (2022): METEO FRANCE, Ineris, Aarhus University, MET Norway, Jülich Research Centre, IEP-NRI, KNMI, TNO, SMHI, FMI, ENEA and BSC.
CAMS ENSEMBLE documentation: https://confluence.ecmwf.int/display/CKB/CAMS%3A+Regional+air+quality+forecast+data+documentation
Global data: CAMS Global Atmospheric Composition Forecasts.

Ozone and PM2.5 are model forecasts in micrograms per cubic metre, not local sensor readings. The available forecast horizon is shorter than the weather forecast. Missing samples are not replaced by zero or extrapolated. Graph colours indicate magnitude, not health-risk categories.
