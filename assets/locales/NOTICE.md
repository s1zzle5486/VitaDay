Date, country, region and time zone names: Unicode CLDR (see ../UNICODE-LICENSE and ../CLDR-LICENSE). New UI and many holiday translations were machine-generated. French holidays include a curated common-holiday dictionary; unknown holiday names fall back to English. Traditional Chinese derives from Simplified Chinese via OpenCC, and European Portuguese adapts Brazilian Portuguese terminology. Native-speaker review is welcome. Translation is performed only during development. The Vita never sends UI strings or personal events to a translation service. Russian UI and holiday translations from previous releases are retained.
