Bitmap CJK glyphs are derived from Noto Sans CJK SC Regular (Adobe).
Generated on the build computer at 48 px by tools/build_cjk_bitmap.py.
Vita uses compressed alpha masks, a Unicode index and its own renderer.
No CJK font parsing or system-language selection is used at runtime.
